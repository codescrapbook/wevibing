We Vibing helps developers learn to code with AI — starting with vibe-driven coding (fast, exploratory, fun), and growing into agentic programming with structure: guardrails, workflows, evaluation, and reliability.

Who it’s for:

- Beginners who want an approachable path into AI-assisted coding
- Practitioners who want practical agent patterns without the hype
- Builders following our “build in public” journey

Everything here (posts and streams) is free for now. If our work helps you, consider supporting us on Patreon. It keeps the lights on and lets us make more.

Thanks for vibing with us.
